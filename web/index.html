Commands

Click a friendly unit to select it, Shift+click to select several, then choose Move, Move selected group, or Attack. WebGL draws GPU 3D block geometry: drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. This is not final art. Construction reserves a site, requires a worker and completes after simulation time. Red units belong to the basic AI opponent.
