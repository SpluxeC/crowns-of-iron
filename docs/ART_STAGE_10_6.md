# Crowns of Iron — Art Stage 10.6: Faction Finish & Smooth Vehicle Clip Transitions

## Implemented

The **120 specialized vehicle, siege, naval and air** models continue to export as deterministic, procedural four-node glTF 2.0 files with one hull and three independently animated rigid attachments. The **96 humanoid skins** remain unchanged.

### Six faction-specific attachment paint finishes
`web/vehicleFinish.js` applies a deterministic six-faction set of metal, trim and wood tints to the three supplementary attachments **before** the glTF color accessors are encoded. Existing original hull textures and geometry are preserved. The six sets are Human, Orc, Dwarf, Tree Elf, Corrupt Elf and Ursa. Values are clamped to [0,1], with a limited brightness response based on the original component color. No external asset fetches or new game statistics are introduced.

### All-node vehicle animation crossfades
`web/vehicleGltf.js` accepts optional `{transitionFrom, transitionAlpha, transitionFromTimeMs}` parameters. It evaluates current and previous clips then blends the hull translation, hull quaternion and all three child quaternions. Quaternion interpolation uses hemisphere-aware normalized interpolation to avoid long-arc reversals. Hit and death remain finite, non-looping clips.

`web/webgl3d.js` keeps a separate bounded, per-visible-unit vehicle clip-transition map and samples all four nodes across a **160 ms blend**. The map is pruned when units disappear from the fog-filtered snapshot. Existing gameplay outcomes, fog of war, hit points and pathfinding are unchanged.

### Testing additions
- Verify all six faction finishes are distinct, bounded and do not change attachment positions or UVs.
- Verify material roles have different shades and reject invalid inputs.
- Verify frame-zero and frame-one crossfade endpoint poses, an intermediate blended pose, all-finite vertices and invalid-transition errors.
- Extend `all-216-art-demo` checks with a crossfade on each of the 120 specialized exported vehicles.
- Include the new module in the syntax check.

## Limits and release gate

This is **first-pass procedural styling and runtime animation blending**, not authored wear masks, baked damage textures, motion-captured animations, independent part aiming from game orders, final PBR, or GPU-certified console rendering. Each specialized vehicle still has only three supplementary moving attachments. The stage must pass Node tests, full export QA and browser visual checks before merging. This is a stacked draft on PR #28; `main` stays untouched.
